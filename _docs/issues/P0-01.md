## Goal

`_docs/specs.md` is internally consistent and reconciled with `_docs/openapi.yaml` and `_docs/ui.md`: all 21 numbered sections are real markdown headings with stable anchors, section 11 (error codes) and section 12 (API summary) match the OpenAPI contract exactly, and no status colour is restated in a form that can contradict `_docs/ui.md`.
